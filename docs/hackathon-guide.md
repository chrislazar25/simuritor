# Base & AITX Talent Hackathon — guide (copied 2026-09-25)
Source: https://common-scooter-829.notion.site/Base-AITX-Talent-Hackathon-3e01e636288e80a7b914c993f90ae6c5
Base Power HQ, 205 E. Riverside Dr, Suite 100, Austin, TX 78704 · Sep 25–27 · In person. Questions: team@aitxcommunity.com
Base engineers judge; Base is hiring in Texas off what gets built.

## Tracks (one project may enter up to 2)
1. Open Grid Data — ERCOT prices, load, generation, congestion. "Show us what you can see in the data that most people miss."
2. Orchestration — coordinate many independent things; "what matters is how it holds up when pieces fail."
3. Most Commercializable — "Base is a power company, not a battery company"; something that could ship on top of what Base does for its members. "Show off your taste."

## Judging (100 pts) — judged off the 5-min demo video + codebase; top per track present live at awards
- Technical Execution & Completeness 30: completeness (core workflow runs without crashing) 15 · technical depth (real engineering, not a wrapper) 15
- Fit to Track 30: attacks the track's problem 15 · articulates the "why" 15
- Value & Impact 20: insight quality (non-obvious, useful) 10 · usability (could Base use it tomorrow?) 10
- Innovation & Execution 20: creativity (novel combo of tools/data; looks and feels good) 10 · performance (optimized for speed or scale) 10
"Real, working systems — not slide decks or simple API wrappers."

## Submission — due Sun Sep 27, 11:00 AM CT, via Airtable, one per team
- Project title
- 2–5 min Loom demo video, camera on, show the core loop live
- Public repo with README: quick start · tech stack + architecture diagram · how to reproduce (env vars, sample .env) · datasets/synthetic data + provenance · known limitations & next steps
- Deployed URL or short screen capture of the working app
- Team roster (names, roles, contacts)
- 150–300 word write-up: problem → who it helps → solution → impact

## Demo video flow (recommended)
1. Team intro (≤30s) 2. Elevator pitch (≤30s: what, who, why — "like a product trailer") 3. Live demo, minimal cuts, show console logs 4. Narrate how it's built (frameworks, internals, challenges) 5. The why / "so what?" and what's next
